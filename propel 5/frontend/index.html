How the calculations work

Each source has a reliability weight. The evidence score is the sum of reliability × age factor × evidence value, divided by the sum of reliability weights. The age factor halves every 90 or 180 days, depending on the skill.

Quality measures source reliability, freshness measures age, and consistency measures agreement. Current policy thresholds are 65%, 60% and 65%. These defaults are experimental, not validated hiring standards.

The planner searches the complete activity catalog for your target. Learning gains and durations are estimates; completing a task never automatically awards a skill score. A new assessment or assessor-reviewed result updates your evidence.
